The Project Path

How a Manuscript Moves Through Our Working Process

Preparing a book for audiences can feel like an unwieldy task. Broken into stages, it becomes manageable — and each stage is one you will see and approve before the next begins.

Every change remains visible, and every decision remains yours. The result is a manuscript shaped through transparent collaboration rather than guesswork.

01

Sample Edit

A short passage is edited so you can evaluate our approach before committing to the full manuscript.

02

Developmental Read

We assess structure, pacing, and clarity at the manuscript level.

03

Style Sheet Creation

Consistent rules for spelling, terminology, and formatting are established and followed throughout.

04

Line Editing

Sentence-level revisions improve readability while preserving voice.

05

Author Review

You review tracked changes and editorial notes, approving or revising as you see fit.

06

Proofreading Pass

A final check for residual errors before the manuscript is considered complete.
