How We Work

We Begin by Understanding Your Manuscript

Editing is an act of interpretation before it is an act of correction. Before altering a single line, our editors study the manuscript's intent — its tone, its audience, and the effect it is reaching for. This starts with a developmental read: examining structure, narrative clarity, characterization, and rhythm to see how the parts cohere. Our observations are shared with you directly, so editorial direction is agreed on before deeper revisions begin.

From there, the work moves line by line — refining diction, tightening syntax, and improving readability without flattening your voice. Every adjustment comes with margin notes and tracked changes, so nothing happens without your visibility. We note what already works, flag what needs reconsideration, and offer alternatives rather than absolutes. Manuscripts typically move through two substantive editorial passes, though the process stays adaptable to what the material needs. The result is a manuscript that reads with intention — coherent, genre-aware, and prepared for its next stage.
