Our Approach

Treating Your Manuscript With the Care It Was Written With

A manuscript represents time, attention, and a particular way of seeing the world — we try not to lose sight of that while editing it. Our editors listen before they revise, taking time to understand what you intend the book to become, in both its broad shape and its smaller details. The people doing this work read widely and take the responsibility of editing someone else's writing seriously.

Our approach in practice: we start with a sample edit and a short consultation to align on goals. We then build a style sheet, track recurring details — characters, timelines, terminology — and accompany every suggestion with a clear rationale. You approve each change. Confidentiality is maintained throughout, deadlines are communicated upfront, and the manuscript you receive at the end still reads as unmistakably yours.
