How We Publish Your Book

Publishing starts with a full manuscript review — checking structure, formatting readiness, and what your title needs before it is retailer-ready. From there, we develop a publishing roadmap: editing passes if needed, interior formatting for print and digital, cover design direction, and a distribution plan matched to your genre and goals.

Once the direction is approved, we move into production. You'll see cover concepts, interior proofs, and metadata drafts at every stage, with room to request changes before anything goes live. When the files are locked, we register your ISBN, upload to every platform on your distribution plan, and confirm your listings are live and accurate. What you get at the end is a publication-readyly published book by our publication-ready book publishers — with full ownership of your rights.
