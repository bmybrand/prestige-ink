Privacy Policy

Prestige Ink LLC respects the trust you place in us when sharing personal details. This notice explains what information we receive, why we use it, and the safeguards applied while it remains in our care.

Details You Choose to Share

We receive information that you submit through consultation requests, project forms, email, telephone calls, or other direct conversations. Those details may include your name, contact information, manuscript needs, service preferences, and materials required to discuss or complete your project.

How Those Details Are Used

Your information helps us answer questions, prepare estimates, coordinate requested services, deliver project updates, process authorized transactions, and improve the way our team supports authors. We use it only for legitimate business purposes connected with your inquiry or engagement.

Storage and Protection

We apply reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational controls intended to reduce unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, loss, or disclosure. No online system can provide an absolute security guarantee, but access is limited to people and providers who need the information for an approved purpose.

Service Providers and Disclosure

Prestige Ink LLC does not sell personal information. We may provide limited details to trusted vendors that support hosting, communications, payments, or project delivery, provided they use the information only for that assigned work. We may also disclose information when required by law or necessary to protect legal rights.

Your Choices

You may ask to review, update, correct, or delete personal information associated with you, subject to applicable legal and recordkeeping requirements. You may also withdraw from promotional messages at any time by using the included unsubscribe option or contacting our team directly.

Contact and Policy Updates

Questions about this notice may be sent to info@prestigeinkllc.com. We may revise this policy as our practices or legal obligations change, and the version published on this page will apply from its stated posting date.
