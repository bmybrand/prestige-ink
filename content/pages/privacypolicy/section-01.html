Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Prestige Ink LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects the trust you place in us when sharing personal details through our website at prestigeinkllc.com. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how it is stored, and how you can contact us about your data.

Information We Collect

When you use our contact forms, quote requests, or consultation pop-ups, we collect the personal information you choose to submit. That typically includes:

  • Name — so we know how to address you and identify your inquiry
  • Email address — so we can reply to your request and send project-related updates
  • Phone number — so our team can reach you by call or SMS when you have consented to that contact

You may also share optional details such as a project description, genre, word count, timeline, manuscript needs, or other materials needed to discuss or complete your project. If you email or call us directly, we may keep the contact details and information you provide in that conversation.

Why We Collect This Information

We use the information from our forms and conversations to:

  • Respond to inquiries and provide free quotes or consultations
  • Coordinate publishing, writing, editing, design, marketing, or audiobook services you request
  • Communicate about timelines, deliverables, payments, and project status
  • Improve how our team supports authors and maintain internal business records

We use personal information only for legitimate business purposes connected with your inquiry or engagement. We do not sell personal information.

How Information Is Stored and Protected

Form submissions and related project communications are stored in our business systems and, where applicable, with trusted service providers that help us host the website, process email or SMS communications, manage leads, or deliver services. Access is limited to people and providers who need the information for an approved purpose.

We apply reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards intended to reduce unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, loss, or disclosure. No online system can guarantee absolute security, so please avoid sending sensitive credentials or payment card numbers through the contact form.

Service Providers and Disclosure

We may share limited personal details with vendors that support hosting, communications, payments, CRM/lead handling, or project delivery, provided they use the information only for that assigned work. We may also disclose information when required by law, to enforce our agreements, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Prestige Ink LLC, our clients, or others.

Your Choices and Rights

You may ask to review, update, correct, or delete personal information associated with you, subject to applicable legal and recordkeeping requirements. You may withdraw from promotional email or SMS messages at any time by using the unsubscribe option, replying STOP where available, or contacting us directly.
